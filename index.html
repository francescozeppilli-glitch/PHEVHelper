Elettrico o benzina?

PHEV Helper confronta il costo stimato per 100 km della guida elettrica e a benzina. Inserisci i consumi della tua auto e scopri in pochi secondi quale alimentazione conviene usare.

Come funziona

  1. Crea il profilo del veicolo. Inserisci consumi elettrici e a benzina nelle unità che usi abitualmente.
  2. Imposta i prezzi. Regola il prezzo della benzina, della ricarica domestica o della colonnina.
  3. Confronta. Visualizza il costo per 100 km, il risparmio stimato e la soglia di convenienza dell’energia.

I tuoi veicoli restano con te

Profili, prezzi e foto opzionali sono salvati localmente sul dispositivo. Non serve creare un account. Leggi l’informativa completa per conoscere i servizi esterni utilizzati dall’app.

English

Know the cost before you leave

Electric or petrol?

PHEV Helper compares the estimated cost per 100 km of electric and petrol driving. Enter your car’s consumption figures and find out which energy source is more convenient in seconds.

See how it works How we handle data

Three comparisons, one clear answer

🗺️

Regional average

In Italy, use the regional average petrol price published by the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy.

🔌

Charging station

Compare the petrol price with the tariff of the charging station you intend to use.

🏠

Home charging

Calculate convenience using the home electricity price saved in your vehicle profile.

How it works

  1. Create your vehicle profile. Enter electric and petrol consumption using your preferred units.
  2. Set current prices. Adjust the petrol, home electricity or charging station price.
  3. Compare. View the cost per 100 km, estimated savings and the electricity break-even price.

Your vehicles stay with you

Profiles, prices and optional photos are stored locally on your device. No account is required. Read the full Privacy Policy to learn about the external services used by the app.
